The enrolment journey

  1. Step 1

    Enquiry

    To experience Claremont College for yourself, book a private tour or attend an Open Day. Our Registrar is available by phone or email to assist you.

  2. Step 2

    Application

    Complete our online enrolment application form. The documents you will need are listed at the start, and a $200 non-refundable application fee applies on submission. Once received, we will confirm by email and add your child to the waitlist for their nominated year of entry. You will receive an interview booking link via email when it is time to schedule your interview.

  3. Step 3

    Interview

    Usually held 12–18 months before commencement, the interview provides an opportunity for us to get to know your family and discuss your child’s education and needs.

  4. Step 4

    Offer of enrolment

    Following your completion of steps 1-3, your application will be carefully reviewed, subject to availability and our Enrolment Policy. All successful applicants will receive a written Letter of Offer.

  5. Step 5

    Accept your offer

    After receiving your Letter of Offer, both parents/carers must sign and pay a non-refundable enrolment fee of $1,800 to secure their child’s place.
