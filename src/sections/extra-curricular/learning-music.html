Learning music

Learning to love music (not only as a listener, but as a practitioner) has a profound impact upon children, which extends far beyond a simple appreciation of the arts. Studies have shown that music education deepens concentration and sharpens memory in both visual and verbal skills. That’s why music is woven throughout life at Claremont College, with tuition from Prep to Year 6 and a range of ensembles where children gain real performance experience. Because when a child discovers what they can do, the confidence follows them everywhere.
