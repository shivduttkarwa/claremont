From the arts, to tech, to games of strategy, our activities develop skills beyond the curriculum.

  • Kickstart their day

    Children don’t have to wait for the school bell to start learning. Our Morning Skills program gives them an active, confident start to the day, with sports like Football, Tennis, Martial Arts, Basketball, AFL and Athletics on offer before school begins (K–6). Programs may vary from year to year.

  • Creative and performing arts

    We inspire creative children to get moving with Dance from REDedDance (K-6), or explore the visual arts with hands-on activities at Class Artz Academy’s Junior Art School (K-6).

  • Coding camp

    At Code Camp, students in Years 2-6 dive into coding, Minecraft engineering, animation, design and robotics.

  • Chess club

    At Chess Club, students sharpen their strategy with coaching from the Sydney Academy of Chess, while lunchtime chess classes prepares keen players to represent Claremont at the annual schools’ competition.

  • Debating

    Through debating, students can build confidence and discover their voice, supported by debating coaching from Masters Academy.
