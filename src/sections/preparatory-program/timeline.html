A day in the life

There’s no such thing as an ‘average day’ at Claremont, but every day shares the same structure. From morning learning to afternoon tea, every part of your child’s day is shaped with care, to ensure they’re happy and engaged from the second they arrive to the moment they leave.

  1. 7am

    TeamKids opens its doors

    Preparatory students may access TeamKids Before School Care from 7:00am. Ideal for families who prefer an early drop off.

  2. 8:15am

    Preparatory student drop off

    Preparatory students are welcomed at the main grounds from 8:15am, or at Wylie Cottage from 8:40am.

  3. 9am

    Morning gathering

    Families are invited to our learning space from 8:40am and may stay until 9:00am, when our students explore their visual schedule and prepare for the day ahead.

  4. 9:15am - 11:15am

    Morning learning

    Lessons will focus on modules like Phonics and Literacy (PreLit) Program, Handwriting, Specialist Lesson (PE, Music, Literature, Library borrowing).

  5. 11:15am - 12:35pm

    Lunch and outdoor play

    Lunch and outdoor playtime allows children to develop their friendships and social skills through active, imaginative play, while enjoying some fresh air.

  6. 12:35pm - 1pm

    Afternoon learning

    After lunch, the lessons will focus on modules like Mathematics, Language and Communication, Social and Emotional Learning, Thinking and Self-regulation.

  7. 1pm - 1:30pm

    Afternoon tea

    During afternoon tea, children spend time sitting and playing together in smaller groups.

  8. 1:30pm - 2:30pm

    Indoor inquiry

    Children work in small groups, exploring purposeful indoor learning contexts, while focusing on their current research inquiry with the support of a staff member.

  9. 2:30pm - 3pm

    End of day activity

    Every day is brought to a close with a visit to our chapel or an end of day gathering with buddies.

  10. 3:00pm - 6:00pm

    Team Kids Open

    Preparatory students will be taken to TeamKids Before and After School Care if they are booked in for the day by Preparatory team at 3:00pm. Parents can pick them up at any time till 6:00pm
