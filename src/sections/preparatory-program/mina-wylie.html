Mina Wylie’s legacy.

Our Wylie Cottage Preparatory bears the name of Mina Wylie, Claremont College alumna, Olympic silver-medallist, and one of only two women to swim for Australia at the 1912 Games.

Mina’s determination and pioneering spirit lives on in the cottage that carries her name. A nurturing environment where children are encouraged to dream big, build confidence, and start their educational journey with purpose.
