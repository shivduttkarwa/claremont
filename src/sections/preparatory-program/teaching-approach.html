Our teaching approach

Every child deserves to feel confident, connected and safe. Our Preparatory Wellbeing Program gives them the tools they need to feel all three.

Kimochis® Social and Emotional Learning helps children to understand and manage their emotions. The program teaches children to respond constructively to challenging situations and make responsible decisions.

Developed by the Office of the Children’s Guardian for children aged 2–6, the SAFE Series teaches personal safety in language a young child can actually use. Children learn what a boundary is, how to notice when something doesn’t feel right, and that asking for help is always the right thing to do.

At Claremont, school readiness begins with our Preparatory Program, designed to provide children with a strong foundation for a successful transition to school. Our educational program is informed by current research and best practice in Early Childhood education, with our units of inquiry drawing on the principles of Reggio Emilia within an Australian context. Our curriculum is designed around a three-week cycle, providing children with diverse indoor and outdoor learning contexts that encourage curiosity, exploration, collaboration and play-based learning.

Our Preparatory students participate in a range of specialist learning experiences that extend their learning and develop their skills, interests and confidence. Specialist classes include PE, Music, Library Literature and Library Borrowing.
