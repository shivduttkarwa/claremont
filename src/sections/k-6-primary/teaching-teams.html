Collaborative teaching teams

Comprised of three to four educators (including classroom teachers, learning enrichment teachers and teaching and learning assistants), our teams work together to provide a tailored, holistic response to each child’s educational and emotional needs. By sharing their unique expertise and combining their diverse perspectives, our collaborative teaching teams are able to ensure that all students are seen, every child is supported, and no child is overlooked.
