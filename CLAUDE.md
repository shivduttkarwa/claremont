## Tokens + units
- Every design value lives in `src/assets/styles/01-settings/_tokens.less` as a `:root` custom property. Custom CSS uses `var(--…)` only; **no px anywhere** (rem, em, %, vw). The 8px scale (`--space-*`) drives mobile and tablet rhythm; at the design width components use the exact Figma value in rem with the px figure in a trailing comment (`5.3125rem; // 85`). Component-specific Figma measures are declared as local custom properties at the top of the block, with the px value in a trailing comment.
- Bootstrap is themed only through `--bs-*` in `_bootstrap-vars.less`; buttons through `--bs-btn-*` in `05-components/_buttons.less`.
- **The root font-size is a px table per breakpoint**, in `html` in `03-base/_base.less` — the one place px is allowed, because every rem resolves against it. Nothing else in the project may use px. 16 holds from 0 to the 1640 design width and is load-bearing there: the Figma audit at 1640, the 44 tap targets (`2.75rem`), the 16 form controls and the 12 text floor are all rem and verified at 16, so changing any value up to 1640 breaks them. One deliberate laptop exception: 14 (0.875×) from 992 to 1399, every pointer (it started at 1200 until Cocogun's slide 11, 2026-10-20: at 992 the nav, icons and quick links had been larger than at 1280 and only the logo shrank, and their 992 × 600 check is DevTools' touch-emulating responsive mode, so a mouse-only rule would not have shown them the change). `mobile-audit.mjs` therefore reports 38.5 tap targets and 13 inputs from 992 to 1399; that is the accepted cost, not a regression. **A complaint about one size is fixed in that size's band only** (the user, 2026-10-21): the slide 11 fixes live in 992–1199, the 14 root, the 1200 bar's header measures and the hero title a tenth smaller there (`7.402 * var(--hero-u)` under `max-width: 1199.98px` in `_hero.less`: 60 at 600 tall); 1200 and up, portrait tablets and phones stayed as approved. Above it the root stays 16 (Cocogun's review, slide 6, 2026-10-20: copy, nav, buttons and logos never grow past their 1640 size; it used to be `calc(100vw / 102.5)`, the frame zoomed to fit). What still grows past 1640: full-bleed photos and colour bands fill the screen at their 1640 proportions, and measures tied to a photo (a banner's height and its copy's top offset, the Mina Wylie split) use `--du` in `_tokens.less`, a rem that becomes `100vw / 102.5` past 1640; the 1240 column centres; the header, footer and the rows of text columns (pillars, Preparatory and K-6 columns) keep the 1640 frame centred through `.design-frame()`; the right-hand banner copy never sits further left than its 57.26% on the photo; the activity cards share the spare width once six no longer fill it, and `pan.js` grows the testimonial and educator cards just enough that the loop covers the screen (2240+ and 2500+). A px root ignores the reader's own browser font setting (browser zoom still works); switching the table to `%` — `100%` for 16, `112.5%` for 18, `131.25%` for 21, `150%` for 24 — renders identically and restores that. After any change here, re-run the Figma audit and `mobile-audit.mjs`: both must be unchanged, because both only ever see 16.
- **Escape fluid maths from LESS**: `~"clamp(1rem, 0.5rem + 2vw, 3rem)"`, `~"max(…)"`, `~"min(…)"`. Unescaped, LESS adds rem + vw into a wrong constant.
- Breakpoints are the LESS vars `@bp-sm … @bp-xxl` (px, matching Bootstrap's grid exactly), mobile-first `min-width` queries. `@upto-lg`, `@upto-md` and `@upto-xxl` are the `max-width` twins for redraws and for Figma measures that only exist at the design width; `@bp-design` (1640) is the frame itself.
- **A fixed Figma measure must still fit a 992 screen.** Where a value only works with the whole 1640 frame, it scales down with `clamp()` and returns to the exact Figma value by 1640 (the pillar and link-column paddings; the header from 992 to 1199 is the 1200 bar, logo 238, links 14, only its nav gap closing from 31.5 to 24.5 between 1013 and 992 so it fits), or the column caps it with container units (`container-type: inline-size` on the item, then `min(…, 20cqw)` for the pillar statement and `calc(100cqw * 475 / 302)` for the news card's height). Never a fixed height where copy can grow: the mosaic rows follow their photos' aspect ratios and the cards stretch, so a long paragraph on a narrow laptop grows the row instead of spilling out.
- Headings wrap naturally (no `text-wrap: balance`): Figma's line breaks are greedy.
